# funcrop 0.1.0

## New features

* Initial release.
* B-spline basis construction with penalty matrices (`bspline_basis()`).
* Tensor product B-splines for 2D functional data (`tensor_bspline_basis()`).
* Mixed model reparameterisation (`make_Zspline()`).
* Dual-backend architecture: ASReml-R v4.2 (REML) and bayesreml (Bayesian MCMC).
* S3 classes: `fda_basis`, `fda_data`, `fda_model`.
